About BhoomiAI

BhoomiAI brings satellite-based farm intelligence directly to your smartphone. Whether you manage a single field or multiple farms, BhoomiAI gives you the insights you need to optimise yields, detect problems early, and make data-driven decisions — all in 12 Indian languages.

Core Features

Farm Geotagging

  • Draw Mode: Tap points on a satellite map to outline your farm boundary
  • Walk Mode: Walk your farm perimeter and let GPS trace the boundary automatically
  • Automatic area calculation in acres

Soil Health

  • Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K), Organic Carbon, and pH readings
  • Visual progress bars colour-coded to optimal ranges
  • PDF report download option

Crop Intelligence

  • Stage-by-stage crop calendar with colour-coded activity timeline
  • Pest and disease forewarning cards with symptoms, spread mode, and solutions
  • NDVI-based crop health tracking via Sentinel-2 satellite data
  • LSWI (Land Surface Water Index) for water stress detection

Irrigation & Weather

  • Surface, sprinkler, and drip irrigation scheduling
  • Soil moisture heatmaps and time-series charts
  • 14-day weather forecast with rainfall and temperature trends

Multi-Farm Management

  • Manage multiple farms across different locations from one account
  • Shared-farm mode for managing farms on behalf of others
  • Community feed for sharing insights with other farmers

Language Support

  • Available in 12 languages: English, Hindi, Kannada, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Gujarati, Assamese, Punjabi, Malayalam, and Odia

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new farm?

On the Home tab, tap Add New. Choose Draw to outline your farm by tapping corners on the satellite map, or Walk to trace the boundary by walking the perimeter with GPS. After confirming the boundary, fill in your crop details and save.

Why does the app ask for location permission?

Location is used only for farm geotagging. Walk mode uses GPS to continuously trace your path; draw mode can optionally use location to centre the map on your current position. You can use draw mode without granting location permission.

What is the demo farm?

When you first open BhoomiAI, a sample farm ("Demo Farm") is pre-loaded so you can explore all features — soil health, crop calendar, NDVI charts, weather, and more — without needing to add your own farm first.

What is included in the premium subscription?

Premium unlocks satellite-powered sections for your own farms: Soil Health, Pest & Disease alerts, Crop Calendar, Irrigation scheduling, Soil Moisture heatmaps, NDVI/Crop Health charts, LSWI tracking, and Weather forecasts. Pricing is ₹700 per acre for a 6-month subscription.

My satellite data looks the same for every farm — why?

BhoomiAI currently uses demonstration data to showcase all features. Real satellite data integration (Sentinel-2 NDVI, MODIS soil moisture, OpenWeatherMap) is the next milestone on our roadmap and will be activated in an upcoming update.

How do I delete a farm?

Open the farm detail screen, tap the menu icon (⋮) in the top-right corner, and select Delete Farm.

I selected the wrong language during setup — how do I change it?

Language selection currently appears during the onboarding flow. To change it, sign out and relaunch the app to access the language selection screen again. A language-change option in Settings is planned for an upcoming release.
