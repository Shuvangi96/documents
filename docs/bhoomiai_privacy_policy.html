Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 19, 2025
Last Updated: May 19, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how BhoomiAI ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Registration Data: First name, last name, phone number, and email address when you create an account
  • Google Sign-In: Basic profile information (name, email) when you choose to sign in with Google
  • Language Preference: Your chosen display language, stored locally on your device

1.2 Farm and Location Data

  • GPS Location: Precise coordinates used to geotag farm boundaries during walk mode and to centre maps during draw mode
  • Farm Polygon Coordinates: The boundary points you draw or walk to define your farm's shape
  • Reverse Geocoding: Approximate place names (district, region) derived from your farm's centroid to display a human-readable location
  • Farm Metadata: Farm name, crop name, variety, farming type, sowing date, and area — entered by you and stored locally on your device

1.3 Device Information

  • Device Identifiers: Device model and operating system version for compatibility
  • Technical Information: App version, crash reports, and basic performance data via Expo Application Services

1.4 Usage Data

  • App Interactions: Screens visited and features used, to help us improve the app experience
  • Error Logs: Anonymous crash and error reports to help us identify and fix issues

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Core App Functionality

  • Create and manage your farm profiles
  • Display satellite-based soil health, crop health (NDVI), and soil moisture data for your farms
  • Show pest and disease forewarnings and crop calendar guidance relevant to your crops
  • Provide irrigation scheduling and 14-day weather forecasts
  • Calculate crop age and sowing-based advisories

2.2 Location Services

  • Trace farm boundaries using GPS in walk mode
  • Centre the map on your current position during boundary drawing
  • Derive a human-readable location name (district, state) for each farm via reverse geocoding

2.3 Authentication

  • Authenticate your identity via email/password or Google OAuth
  • Maintain your logged-in session across app restarts using local device storage

2.4 App Improvement

  • Analyse anonymous usage patterns to improve features and performance
  • Fix bugs and technical issues identified through crash reports

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party services to provide and improve our app:

3.1 Expo Application Services (EAS)

  • Purpose: App building, over-the-air updates, and basic analytics
  • Data Collected: Device information, app usage statistics
  • Privacy Policy: expo.dev/privacy

3.2 Google OAuth (expo-auth-session)

  • Purpose: Providing "Sign in with Google" functionality
  • Data Collected: Name and email address from your Google account, only when you choose to sign in with Google
  • Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

3.3 Google Maps (react-native-maps)

  • Purpose: Displaying satellite map tiles for farm boundary drawing and viewing
  • Data Collected: Map tile requests may include your approximate location
  • Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

4. Data Storage

4.1 Local Device Storage

Most of your data never leaves your device. Farm profiles, crop details, account information, and your language preference are all stored locally using AsyncStorage on your device.
  • Farm polygon coordinates, crop data, and sowing dates are stored only on your device
  • Your account credentials are stored locally in an encrypted format — we do not maintain a remote user database at this time
  • All locally stored data is removed when you uninstall the app

4.2 Future Cloud Storage

  • In a future release, farm data will be synced to a secure backend to enable multi-device access and satellite data integration
  • We will update this Privacy Policy and notify you before any such change

5. Location Permissions

5.1 When We Request Location Access

  • Foreground Location: Requested when you open the map-draw or map-walk screens to geotag a farm boundary
  • Background Location: Requested to allow continuous GPS tracking during the boundary-walk flow even if the app is briefly backgrounded

5.2 Your Control

  • You can deny location permission — the draw-mode (tap to mark points) works without GPS; only walk mode requires location access
  • You can revoke location permission at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy → Location Services
  • We do not share your raw GPS coordinates with any third party

6. Data Retention

  • Farm Data: Retained on your device until you delete the farm or uninstall the app
  • Account Data: Retained on your device until you sign out or uninstall the app
  • Analytics Data: Retained for up to 26 months by Expo Application Services
  • Crash Reports: Retained for 90 days for debugging purposes

7. Your Rights and Choices

7.1 Access and Control

  • Edit or delete any farm directly within the app
  • Sign out at any time to clear your session from the device
  • Delete all local data by uninstalling the app
  • Control location permissions through your device settings

7.2 Subscription Management

  • Manage or cancel your BhoomiAI premium subscription through your Apple App Store or Google Play Store account settings
  • Access continues until the end of your current billing period after cancellation

7.3 Data Requests

You may contact us to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data
  • Object to processing of your personal data

8. Children's Privacy

BhoomiAI is intended for use by adult farmers and agricultural professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

9. International Users

BhoomiAI is primarily designed for Indian farmers and is available globally. If you are located outside India, please note that your information may be processed in India or in the country where our third-party service providers operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for any international data transfers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or for legal compliance. We will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Notify users of significant changes through an in-app notice
  • Obtain fresh consent for material changes that affect your rights
